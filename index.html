Novartis Oncology Data Science Internship

research · May – Aug 2025

Integrated bulk RNA-seq data with tumorigenicity outcomes to identify transcriptomic features linked to successful tumor formation, and trained a machine learning model to prioritize cell lines by their in vivo tumor-forming potential.

What I learned

  • Differential gene expression and gene set enrichment analysis
  • Machine learning: random forest, Naive Bayes, logistic regression, and linear regression
  • Scanpy for single-cell RNA-seq data analysis

Prizes received

Rouzbeh Amini Lab: Tricuspid Valve Mechanics

research · Jun – Aug 2022 · published in JBE

Researched the mechanical properties of the tricuspid valve in porcine hearts through the Young Scholars Program at Northeastern University. Used biaxial mechanical testing and stress-strain plots to characterize changes in stiffness over time. Resulted in a publication in the Journal of Biomechanical Engineering in May 2023.

What I learned

  • Porcine heart dissection
  • Biaxial mechanical testing, sterile technique, and suturing
  • MATLAB for biomechanics data analysis
